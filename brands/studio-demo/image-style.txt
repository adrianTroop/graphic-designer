Fictional Studio Demo direction: minimal editorial compositions with simple geometric forms, generous negative space and one clear focal point. Use the configured Ink, Paper and Lime palette with restrained accents and clean edges. If photography is requested, use natural proportions, soft daylight and uncluttered backgrounds. Leave room for separately editable text. Avoid baked-in captions, invented logos, watermarks and fake documentary scenes. The bundled demo uses no photography.
