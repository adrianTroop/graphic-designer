KAI TAK SPORTS PARK - TRANSPARENT ASSET PACK
27 October 2026

USE
PNG: place into a design, presentation or document. Transparency is in the alpha channel; a checkerboard is not baked in.
SVG: scalable editable paths for vector assets. The three PNG-only source logos are raster, not vector.
Keep the logo proportions. Preserve the supplied lockup. Put colour logos over light backgrounds and white marks over a sufficiently dark background.

FILES
01_Logos: high-resolution colour primary logo; native-size white logo; Sports Park + Mall lockup; Friends of KTSP vector adaptations.
02_Social_Platform_Marks: original website-supplied social marks, SVG and PNG. Some contain an intentional brand tile or white glyph inside the transparent canvas.
03_Service_Icons: 14 newly drawn service/highlight symbols, each in gradient red and white. Each has SVG + 1024px PNG.
04_Graphic_Overlays: court lines, event-card arc, diagonal strips, and red/purple frames with transparent centres.
05_Layout_Components: separate white panels and grey information band.

PROVENANCE
Source raster: original pixels and alpha retained. The large colour logo is archived on Wikimedia Commons, whose source is KTSP; it was visually compared to the live official website logo. The Mall lockup is from a published partner source. The white logo comes from the official venue capacity PDF.
Source vector adaptation: Friends of KTSP lettering paths retained; the website UI pill removed. The white variant is a placement adaptation, not independently confirmed as an approved variant.
Redrawn component: a working reconstruction based on the inspected public social design family, not official master artwork.
See asset-manifest.json and index.html for each source, status and size.

SIZE LIMITS
Primary colour PNG: 5071 x 2284.
Sports Park + Mall PNG: 2000 x 399.
White primary PNG: 302 x 136. Do not enlarge beyond its useful raster size. Suggested maximum: 151px at 2x density, or 25.6mm wide at 300ppi. A larger approved white/vector logo master remains needed for large placement.
SVG icons and motifs scale cleanly. Their raster exports are 1024px icons and the stated 1080px canvases.

LAYER ORDER
Colour field / photo > court or diagonal motif > optional white panel > logo > icon > Chinese headline > English support > event facts > notice.
The red and purple frames have empty centres. Their matching white panels are separate files. Overlays are not finished posters.

SCOPE
This pack covers the reusable KTSP identity elements documented in the guide and available public source marks. It is not a complete official master library. Dedicated Stadium, Arena and Youth Sports Ground marks, a standalone Mall wordmark, official fonts, exact master motif files and third-party event/sponsor artwork were not recovered. Use the supplied promoter assets for those identities; do not recreate their logos from screenshots.

The native brand assets remain the property of their respective owners. This handoff does not represent KTSP brand approval.
