# Studio Demo — design guide

This is a fictional brand used to demonstrate a reusable design workflow. Every rule below is a proposed demo choice, not an official standard for a real company. Use this identity only for Studio Demo examples.

## Tone and writing
Write clear, concise copy in sentence case. Lead with one strong headline. Keep supporting text practical, direct and easy to scan. Use one CTA only when an action is relevant. Do not invent claims or imply a real event, endorsement or commercial offer. Demonstration artwork must be recognisable as a sample.

## Identity
This demo has no logo. Use the brand name as plain editable text where identification is useful. Do not invent a mark or borrow another company's assets. Keep all identity text separate from backgrounds, images, shapes and captions. If a future brief supplies a logo, use its original transparent SVG or PNG and preserve its proportions; do not redraw it silently.

## Colour
Use Ink as the main colour, Paper for light backgrounds and Lime for selective emphasis. Use the separately configured HEX values as the source of truth. Portrait and square examples use Paper backgrounds with Ink type. Wide examples can use an Ink background with Paper type. Reserve Lime for a small label, a CTA panel or a simple accent. Keep the palette restrained and check contrast for each text/background pairing.

## Typography
Use the configured heading and body fonts. Both currently use generic sans-serif; the final typeface is platform-dependent and is not a verified custom font. Use large, bold headlines, smaller supporting text and a compact eyebrow or label. Prefer left alignment. Avoid decorative type, outlines and shadows. Keep reading text legible at the final display size; shorten or recompose before shrinking text excessively.

## Composition
Use generous outer margins and a clear vertical rhythm. Separate eyebrow, headline, body, details, CTA and sample label into independent editable layers. Keep a dominant headline, a restrained supporting text area and one accent. The full-width background is a separate layer. Do not merge the entire composition into a single working image.

## Static publication families
- Portrait announcement: 1080 × 1350 pixels, 4:5. Light background. Eyebrow above a large headline, followed by body, details and an optional CTA.
- Square card: 1080 × 1080 pixels, 1:1. Light background. Reduce copy to keep a strong headline and clear supporting information.
- Landscape graphic: 1600 × 900 pixels, 16:9. Dark background. Recompose the same information for a wide frame; do not stretch the portrait design.
These are demonstration canvases, not verified requirements for any social network. Only the headline is required in these templates. Omit an unused CTA and its panel together. Keep the visible DESIGN SAMPLE label in demo exports; factual drafts use DRAFT / REVIEW.

## Additional publication formats
For a carousel, design separate cover, content and closing cards with consistent hierarchy and progression; inspect every card. For vertical stories, short-video covers or platform banners, confirm the destination's current dimensions, crops and interface safe areas. Motion, carousel assembly and channel-specific publishing are not provided by the bundled static renderer. Build and review a suitable template before calling a new format supported.

## Images and graphic elements
The included demo artwork is typography and simple geometric shapes, with no photographs or external assets. Keep decorative elements sparse. If the brief adds imagery, follow the separate Image style field, preserve subject proportions and leave clear space for editable copy. Do not generate fake documentary evidence, watermarks or logos inside images. Keep photos, cutouts, icons and shapes as separate assets.

## Slides, documents, charts and web pages
Carry the Ink/Paper/Lime palette and heading/body roles across outputs. Use one main idea per slide, readable source notes and consistent margins. Use document styles for headings, paragraphs, captions and tables. Charts need accurate values, labelled units and sources; combine colour with labels or other distinctions. Web layouts must reflow for narrow screens without clipping text or controls. These are art-direction rules; use an appropriate tool to produce the requested file type.

## Review and handoff
Inspect the complete output at its intended size. Check hierarchy, copy, claims, language, line breaks, fonts, contrast, crop, spacing and any transparent edges. Preserve editable layers and export the required final files. Supply source and revision records. A mechanical pass does not constitute visual or factual approval. Keep revisions distinct and do not publish merely because a draft was generated.
